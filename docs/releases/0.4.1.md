## 简体中文

**CrossDiff 0.4.1 · 图片手动对齐**

- 左右图片各自提供大小（10–400%）和旋转滑块，可直接输入百分比与角度。
- 拖动图片调整位置，对齐裁剪、缩放或旋转后的内容；每侧可独立重置。
- 并排、叠加、滑动对比和像素差异共用对齐结果，切换标签保留当前参数。
- 像素差异新增默认关闭的“仅比较重叠区域”，方便核对裁剪图与原图的共同部分。

调整只影响预览，不修改原文件。目前采用手动对齐；预览最长边为 1600 像素，重采样或压缩可能产生细微差异。对齐参数不在重启后恢复。

需要 macOS 14+。发行应用面向 Apple silicon（arm64），仅有 ad-hoc 签名，尚无 Developer ID 签名与公证。首次打开方式、源码构建和功能边界见 [中文 README](https://github.com/JunyangZhangUSTC/CrossDiff/blob/v0.4.1/README.md)。下载后可用附带的 `SHA256SUMS` 核对附件。

## English

**CrossDiff 0.4.1 · Manual image alignment**

- Adjust each image independently with Scale (10–400%) and Rotation sliders, or enter percentages and angles directly.
- Drag images into position to align cropped, resized, or rotated content. Reset either side independently.
- Side by Side, Overlay, Wipe, and Pixel Difference share the alignment; switching tabs retains the current adjustments.
- Enable the new **Compare Overlap Only** option in Pixel Difference to examine the shared area of a crop and its original. It is off by default.

Adjustments affect previews only; source files stay unchanged. Alignment is manual. Previews are limited to 1600 pixels on the longest edge, and resampling or compression may introduce small differences. Alignment is not restored after restarting the app.

Requires macOS 14+. The distributed app targets Apple silicon (arm64) and is ad-hoc signed, without Developer ID signing or notarization. See the [English README](https://github.com/JunyangZhangUSTC/CrossDiff/blob/v0.4.1/README.en.md) for first-launch instructions, source builds, and limits. Verify downloaded assets using the accompanying `SHA256SUMS`.
