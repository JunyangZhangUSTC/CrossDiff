## 简体中文

**CrossDiff 0.4.2 · 图片四角缩放与翻转**

- 拖动图片四角调整大小，对角保持固定；默认锁定长宽比。
- 每侧可独立解锁比例，通过手柄或宽度、高度滑块与数值框进行非等比伸缩；再次锁定保留当前比例。
- 新增每侧水平、垂直翻转按钮，配合旋转和位置调整对齐图片。
- 四种图片比较模式共用调整结果；单侧重置恢复大小、角度、位置、翻转和比例锁。

所有调整只影响比较预览，不修改原文件。采用手动对齐，不支持透视或自由扭曲；预览最长边为 1600 像素，重采样或压缩可能产生细微差异。调整参数不在重启后恢复。

需要 macOS 14+。发行应用面向 Apple silicon（arm64），仅有 ad-hoc 签名，尚无 Developer ID 签名与公证。首次打开方式、源码构建和功能边界见 [中文 README](https://github.com/JunyangZhangUSTC/CrossDiff/blob/v0.4.2/README.md)。下载后可用附带的 `SHA256SUMS` 核对附件。

## English

**CrossDiff 0.4.2 · Corner resizing and image flips**

- Resize an image by dragging its corners, keeping the opposite corner fixed. Aspect ratio is locked by default.
- Unlock either image to stretch its width and height independently with corner handles, sliders, or numeric fields. Relocking keeps the current proportions.
- Flip either image horizontally or vertically, alongside existing rotation and position adjustments.
- All four comparison modes share the adjustments. Reset either side to restore its size, angle, position, flip states, and aspect lock.

Adjustments affect previews only; source files stay unchanged. Alignment is manual, without perspective or free-form warping. Previews are limited to 1600 pixels on the longest edge, and resampling or compression may introduce small differences. Adjustments are not restored after restarting the app.

Requires macOS 14+. The distributed app targets Apple silicon (arm64) and is ad-hoc signed, without Developer ID signing or notarization. See the [English README](https://github.com/JunyangZhangUSTC/CrossDiff/blob/v0.4.2/README.en.md) for first-launch instructions, source builds, and limits. Verify downloaded assets using the accompanying `SHA256SUMS`.
